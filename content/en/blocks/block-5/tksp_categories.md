Colb’s TKSPs are categorized based on the underlying assets.

## Colb Fund-linked Tokens (CFTs)

CFTs are a derivative form intricately linked to the performance of select funds. They provide
effortless access to premium investments without minimum ticket requirements, serving as a gateway
to a number of international opportunities without the need for traditional banking channels. Tracking
the performance of designated funds, Colb pioneered a C1 Black TKSP that was directly tied to a
BlackRock fund, exemplifying the potential and reliability of CFTs.

![CFTs Overview](/images/blocks/block-5/CFT.png)

## Colb Managed Tokens (CMTs)

Involve active management by a financial expert, and provide strategies aligned to a chosen risk
profile. They represent a product actively managed by a wealth manager in Switzerland. Expanding
upon the success of the tokenized Blackrock fund, CMTs are introduced in collaboration with NS
Partners. Distinguished by three risk profiles - Conservative, Balanced, and Growth - it empowers
investors with diversified portfolio options tailored to their individual risk appetites and investment
goals.

![Indicative Portfolio Allocation of CMTs Underlying Strategies](/images/blocks/block-5/CMT.png)

## Colb Tracker Tokens (CTTs)
Tracking the performance of various assets or financial strategies. Scheduled for introduction in 2024,
they will monitor thematic strategies and private equities. Ideal for investors seeking active
involvement in crafting their portfolios with the goal to define a desired risk/return profile, the CTTs
offer unparalleled opportunities. They grant seamless access to exclusive pre-IPO investment offers,
enabling investors to capitalize on early-stage opportunities with ease. The pioneering offering will be
an OpenAI shares allocation, delivered in collaboration with Stableton.
