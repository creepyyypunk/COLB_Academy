In the real world, TKSPs are represented by a Legal Trust, a fiduciary arrangement that holds the
underlying assets and manages them according to the Trust's terms. The Trust protects the assets
underlying the TKSP and the community’s interests. It establishes a bankruptcy remote structure,
effectively segregating the operational risks of the technology company from the underlying assets. A
regulated trustee is appointed to oversee the trust, mandated to adhere to the trust terms and fiduciary
duties, thereby ensuring responsible asset management and governance. Colb's tokens are regulated
by the Swiss Blockchain Act. They hold the asset's status as ledger-based security. This enables users
to claim ownership, distinguishing these tokens from simple contractual representations of RWAs.
