The gateway to the ecosystem of Colb is $USC. It facilitates issuance & redemption of TKSPs, playing
a fundamental role in automating operations.However, $USC will not serve as a hurdle for investors to
get an exposure to a tokenized investment product. To be fully compliant with the philosophy of DeFi
and money legos, Colb’s decentralized autonomous organization (Colb DAO) will enable multi-currency
access to TKSPs. In addition to established blue-chip cryptocurrencies and stablecoins, Colb will
facilitate direct USD investments in structured products on the platform.
