Colb’s structured investment tokens are called Tokenized Structured Products (TKSPs). The TKSP
framework enables seamless access to the world's premier wealth and asset management strategies
through tokenization, thereby eliminating usual banking constraints.

Traditionally, structured products combine bonds or shares with derivatives, resulting in stand-alone
products certified and issued by an issuer, usually a bank. For Colb, TKSP represents the most
powerful investment vehicle ever invented to scale the origination of real-world assets (RWA).

Their core strength lies in their flexibility to underlie any market asset independently, without the
requirement for external collaboration with the fund or asset itself. It offers the opportunity to access
any asset at scale, whether single or blended, empowering Colb to tokenize every fund, strategy, or
asset available in the market while minimizing legal and operational obstacles.
