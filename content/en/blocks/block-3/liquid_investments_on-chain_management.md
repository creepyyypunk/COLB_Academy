The Colb app provides users easy access to premium assets and wealth management strategies
without the traditional constraints and costs. Users can digitize their wealth by converting USD into
Colb native stablecoin (via bank or swap), which then acts as a USD equivalent – on-chain. Holders
can then deploy the stablecoin or a number of whitelisted currencies (including USD) in private pools
to acquire positions in tokenized structured investment tokens that are underpinned by real-world
assets (RWA) or wealth management strategies.

![App](/images/blocks/block-3/app.png)

Colb’s technological infrastructure is deployed in a non-custodial environment. This implies that all
smart contracts are directly accessible on the blockchain, regardless of the front end of Colb’s
application. Additionally, user authentication within the application is enabled through a wallet
connection, allowing on-chain trading of all tokens. Seamlessness of interaction will be guaranteed
with account abstraction, enabling access of non-crypto native users via email login.
