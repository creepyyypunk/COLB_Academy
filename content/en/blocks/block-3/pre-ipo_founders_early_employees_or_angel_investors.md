Colb’s tokenization framework facilitates angel investors and entrepreneurs to seamlessly convert
shares from private investments or their own companies into structured investment tokens within the
Colb ecosystem. Leveraging the capabilities of the blockchain technology and smart contracts, this
innovative approach unlocks instant liquidity solutions for startup equity holdings. Moreover, it grants
access to capital from the secondary market, a realm previously dominated by institutional
over-the-counter deals. With Colb, angel investors and entrepreneurs can navigate the investment
landscape with newfound flexibility and efficiency, propelling their ventures to greater heights.
