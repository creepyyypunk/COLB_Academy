The technology empowers wealth and asset managers to convert services into structured investment
tokens and distribute them at scale. They can tokenize with Colb by structuring financial strategies or
assets as an investment product. Colb adjusts or approves the structuring, tokenizes it and makes it
available through the Colb app to the community on-chain.

Development in progress on the app allows users to activate a function authorizing a wealth manager
to manage investor holdings on-chain (a decentralized wealth management delegation mandate).
Investors delegate the management of their digital assets to a professional, who will then allocate
them in Colb products alongside other DeFi strategies. All interactions occur on-chain through digital
wallets and can be revoked by the user at any time. The Colb app user’s dashboard will allow investors
to monitor its holdings.

As a first example of a Minimum Viable Product (MVP), the Colb community was offered a structured
investment token, issued in 2022 and redeemed in 2023 – BlackRock BGF World Technology Fund
Class D2 USD Tracker Certificate. In the scenario of rising underlying assets expectations over the
long-term, the Tracker Certificate as indicatively depicted in the graph below offers a full participation
in the performance of the underlying, reflecting its price moves 1:1 (adjusted by conversion ratio and
any related fees).
