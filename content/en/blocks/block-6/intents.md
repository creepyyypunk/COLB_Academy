An intent represents the desired outcome that a user aims to achieve, differing from traditional
transactions that focus on execution details. In blockchain terms, the intent-based order and offer
mechanism matches a user's liquidity provision intention with another user's asset liquidation
intention – based on both their expressed desires. Users convey market intentions by specifying the
order parameters. Successful execution requires parameters signed by both parties to match and be
verified on-chain by an Executor contract, laying the groundwork for direct market listing (or an OTC
deal), Dutch and English auctions.
