Tokenized real-world assets suffer from liquidity issues. Colb plans to increase liquidity for TKSP by
applying a declarative paradigm, where liquidity intents are transparently submitted to the system and
subsequently matched by solvers. An intent represents the desired end state that a user aims to
achieve. Unlike traditional transactions that focus on how an action should be performed, an intent
emphasizes what the desired outcome should be. Colb will onboard a network of on-chain liquidity
providers and enable the provision of off-chain capital from financial institutions at the same time.
