As already mentioned, TKSP users can find natural liquidity in two ways in the Colb ecosystem: by (i)
waiting for the final maturity of the product or (ii) requesting early redemption by fully or partially
liquidating the underlying collateral.

In the first (i) case, most TKSP pools will follow this standard: through the liquidation of the underlying
assets, liquidity will be generated and distributed to the pool participants in $USC. For the second case
(ii), some constraints may arise for TKSP pools with multiple participants. Complete or partial
liquidation of the underlying collateral may not be feasible if it could negatively impact other
participants in the same pool or if the underlying assets are illiquid, such as non-listed assets, private
equity, or real estate.

In such cases, Colb will implement an intent-based system to apply the best effort to search for and
match liquidity intentions for the TKSP on-chain and off-chain.
