Solvers facilitate offer and order matching through an intent-driven delegated execution framework.
Their role is to identify a computational pathway, whether on-chain or off-chain, to achieve the
predefined outcome specified by the user. Once a feasible pathway is identified, solvers compete with
others for the winning solution. Becoming a ‘Solver’ in the system requires a designated stake of Colb
token ($COLB), explained below, associated with the respective account to enable the matching
process. Compensation for Solvers is provided in $COLB, encouraging competition and optimal price
discovery while securing the privilege to execute user intents. Solvers execute on-chain transactions,
matching both sides and transferring the TKSP. Users can specify the fraction of TKSP that the solver
will receive as compensation for their service.
