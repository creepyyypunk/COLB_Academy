Colb was built with the specific goal to democratize access to premium investments. For this reason,
its mission is deeply rooted in values of transparency, trust, and the aspiration to deliver a future where
every individual has equitable access to previously impossible to reach structured products. Colb
envisions an ecosystem where every stakeholder has a fair say in navigating this novel financial
system by leveraging the blockchain infrastructure.
