Staking of $COLB will capture protocol fees within the Colb ecosystem. All $COLB holders have the
option to stake their tokens. It will be a prerequisite for accessing premium assets as part of a tiered
structure. For example, when subscribing to a limited TKSP offer, a percentage of the investment can
be supplied to purchase and integrate $COLB in the given strategy. $COLB will be staked to generate
yield and unlocked at maturity. Redeeming earlier may lose the staked amount. Colb DAO can adjust
the percentage reward and conditions depending on the tokenized product at hand.
