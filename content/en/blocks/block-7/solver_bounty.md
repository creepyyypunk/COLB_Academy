Solvers are rewarded with bounties upon the successful matching of liquidity for secondary market
deals. A bounty represents a percentage of the amount for the requested transaction. The bigger the
selected percentage by the TKSP holder, the higher his chances of receiving capital in a timely manner.

$COLB should be staked in order for an entity or holder to become a Solver of intents. The larger the
staked amount, the higher the chance of securing premium intent deals. Solvers’ stake balances
increase upon successful resolution of intents, and they receive rewards based on the value of the
deal. However, bad behavior leads to penalties, potentially resulting in partial or complete loss of the
staked amount.
