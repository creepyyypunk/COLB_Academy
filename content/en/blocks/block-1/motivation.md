Bitcoin and Ethereum laid the foundations for individuals to access hard to reach investment
opportunities through a novel infrastructure. This inclusion enabled participation in the dynamics of
the digital assets world, yielding significant capital gains for newcomers. It signifies a seismic change
from the pre-blockchain period, where physical assets and currencies were the predominant forms of
wealth representation. In the era of Wealth 3.0, affluence is evolving through cryptocurrencies and
other virtual forms of value. This landscape sees assets digitized and decentralized, facilitated by
tokenization and smart contracts, fundamentally reshaping traditional wealth concepts into a
technology-driven model.

Despite this systemic change, challenges persist:
1. Making traditional assets accessible to individuals, particularly those with digital wealth like
cryptocurrency, remains a hurdle.
2. Independent financial professionals struggle to create and distribute products digitally,
hindered by constraints within traditional finance (TradFi). Banking systems, with their inherent
territoriality, exclusivity, and bureaucracy, pose significant obstacles. Navigating TradFi is
challenging, with the best opportunities and products often reserved for the wealthiest, while a
chain of intermediaries inflates costs.
3. Colb offers a pioneering model where blockchain technology merges with the best aspects of
TradFi. By shielding users from the need to interact with the banking system, Colb provides
access to premium investment opportunities directly on-chain. Employing a non-custodial
protocol with robust legal foundations, Colb delivers state-of-the-art, scalable distribution of
investment tokens linked to premium real-world assets (RWA) through smart contracts.

In this model, tokenized structured products have real-world assets as underlies, complemented by a
stablecoin that eliminates the need for a traditional bank account. Both are interoperable with smart
contracts, facilitating an automated and transparent financial system that is cost-effective and
time-efficient.

The future of investment lies in tokenization, and Colb ensures accessibility for all.
