The Colb Intent-solver protocol operates through a sequence of automated steps, guided by the user
input regarding intent - a desired outcome of a transaction. Colb intent mechanism revolves around
expressing specific goals or objectives related to the liquidity of TKSP and asking for the Solvers to
build a feasible way to make these goals a real transaction on the blockchain.

The model operates through the following steps:
1. Initiates a fulfillment attempt among participants within the same TKSP pool, prioritizing the
search for matching intents among TKSP holders. This process incorporates the right of first
refusal in every TKSP selling transaction.
2. If the attempt is not fully matched or only partially matched, the mechanism proceeds to a
second attempt within deep DeFi, searching for a match across multiple chains.
3. If the attempt remains unmatched or is only partially matched, the mechanism then seeks
liquidity off-chain, utilizing a network of brokers, groups, or financial institutions to fulfill the
intent.
