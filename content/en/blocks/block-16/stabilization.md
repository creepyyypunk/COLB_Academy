Colb Trust holds cash and cash-equivalent assets (primarily US Treasury Bills) as capital reserve to provide liquidity to $USC.

The capital reserve acts as a stability mechanism. Each existent $USC in circulation will be backed at a 1:1 ratio.

If a user requests Colb Trust to issue $USC and makes a fiat deposit, Colb Trust will increase the circulating supply with freshly minted coins, backed by the deposit and minted directly on the user’s non-custodial wallet.

The same applies to the reverse process: $USC can be exchanged for USD, removed from circulation and destroyed.

The Colb Trust bank account balance should always match the total number of $USC in circulation. In other words, at any given time, the balance of assets held in the Colb Trust reserves will be equal to (or greater than) the market capitalisation of $USC.

Colb will implement third-party oracles to periodically check the match between the $USC reserves and the amount in circulation, which are transparently available to the public. Therefore, anyone will be able to check if Colb Trust is solvent and able to provide liquidity to the market.
