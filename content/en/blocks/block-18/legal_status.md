$USC is classified as a currency-backed stablecoin and payment token. Under Swiss law, $USC represents a deposit (debt of the Trust) to the $USC holder, mainly deriving from the redemption rights of the holders.

$USC is not a security in the sense of FinSA and according to FINMA’s ‘Supplement to the guidelines for inquiries regarding the regulatory framework for initial coin offerings (ICOs)’.

Colb Trust is exempted from the requirement to obtain a banking licence pursuant to ‘article. 5 (3) (f) Banking and Savings Institutions Ordinance (as of 30 April 2014)’.
