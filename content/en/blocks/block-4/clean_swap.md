access $USC with their crypto, making possible a completely bankless experience. $USC 'Clean Swap'
marks a pioneering leap in the crypto market, embodying Colb’s commitment to unparalleled
transparency and trust. Every interaction with $USC within Colb’s web app, whether buying, selling or
swapping adheres to Swiss regulatory standards.
