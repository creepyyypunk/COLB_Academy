Launched in 2023, $USC is the premier Swiss-compliant stablecoin pegged one-to-one with the USD.
$USC is the access key to the Colb ecosystem and functions as a replacement for a bank account,
automating TKSP subscription. It embodies regulatory compliance and seamless security.

Alongside being a relevant settlement method in the tokenization process for TKSPs, $USC has
several advantages which uniquely position it within the stablecoin landscape. Notably, the full
transparency with information on entities that are custodians, counterparties, or bank account
providers of $USC reserves. Also, it is impossible for any third party (including Colb and $USC Trust) to
remove, freeze, transfer, destroy or perform any action on any user's wallet balance.

Other features include reserves deposited and managed by the $USC USD Issuer Trust ($USC Trust)
through a management mandate (facilitating best execution of orders for the reserves’ portfolio) and a
full bank guarantee issued on the reserves.

Users can access $USC by depositing USD in the $USC Trust Treasury or swapping it against other
cryptocurrencies, such as MATIC and Tether, with Colb. $USC is also available on the DeFi and CeFi
crypto exchanges.
