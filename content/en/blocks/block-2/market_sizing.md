Younger generations, including Millennials and Gen Z, exhibit distinct investment behaviors compared
to Baby Boomers and Gen X. They are expected to inherit an estimated US$8.6tn of global HNW
wealth by 2030 and US$84tn over the next two decades. These generations show a higher preference
for private and digital assets, prioritize ethical investments and rely heavily on social media and digital
channels.

They are also more inclined towards industries like media, tech, and telecommunications, diverging
from older generations in traditional sectors. Furthermore, younger generations, facing challenges
such as recessions and rising housing costs, are more open to alternative financial systems and
investments, including crypto and blockchain-based services. Statistics indicate that 31% of younger
individuals own crypto compared to 12% of older demographics, with the majority of crypto buyers
falling in the 18-40 age range, primarily comprising Gen Z and Millennials.
